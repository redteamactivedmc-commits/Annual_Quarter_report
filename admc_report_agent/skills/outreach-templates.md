# Outreach Templates

Use these as defaults. Adapt to the source and target outlet.

## 1. Standard media pitch

Subject line options:
1. [issue or relevance]
2. [data point or finding]
3. [spokesperson or access angle]

Body:

Hi [name],

I thought this may be relevant because [why it matters now].

[One short paragraph on the angle, tied to the target market or outlet interest.]

[One short paragraph with support such as data, access, event timing, or a concrete example.]

If useful, I can share [interview access / a short comment / more detail].

Best,
[name]

## 2. Interview pitch

Hi [name],

Given the current focus on [issue], we can offer an interview with [spokesperson, title] on [specific angle].

The conversation would be most useful for [audience reason], especially because [timely support].

If this is of interest, I can send a few topic lines or arrange a short slot.

Best,
[name]

## 3. Follow up note

Hi [name],

Following up in case this is useful.

The clearest hook here is [single strongest reason]. We can also offer [one specific value add].

Happy to send a short note or set up a quick interview if relevant.

Best,
[name]

## 4. Reactive comment pitch

Hi [name],

In response to [event or development], [spokesperson, title] can share a short comment on [tight angle].

The key point is [one sentence insight].

If useful, I can send the quote today.

Best,
[name]

## 5. Byline pitch

Hi [name],

I wanted to suggest a contributed article on [argument or issue], tailored for your audience.

The piece would focus on [2 to 3 concrete discussion points], with a clear angle on [market, sector, or operational issue].

If this fits your plans, I can share a short outline.

Best,
[name]

## Subject line patterns

Use these patterns when drafting options:
- issue first: [issue]: a view on [impact]
- data first: new [finding] on [market or risk]
- access first: interview opportunity on [topic]
- event first: around [event], a view on [issue]

Avoid subject lines that sound like release headlines.
