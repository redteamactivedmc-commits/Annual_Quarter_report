# Regional Fit Checks

Use this file to decide whether a source really has UAE, KSA, or GCC relevance.

## Core test

A regional angle is strong only when at least one of these is true:
- the source includes local data, local impact, or local operational relevance
- the issue connects credibly to a sector priority in the target market
- the timing matches a real market, policy, or investment discussion in the region
- the spokesperson can comment on a regional problem in a grounded way

If none of these hold, say the local angle is weak.

## UAE fit signals

Stronger when the topic links to:
- enterprise digitization and operational efficiency
- critical infrastructure, finance, telecom, government, transport, or smart services
- cloud, ai, cybersecurity, data, logistics, or customer experience priorities
- regulatory, resilience, or risk management conversations with clear business impact

Weak when the pitch depends only on broad claims like innovation, leadership, or transformation with no operational substance.

## KSA fit signals

Stronger when the topic links to:
- scale, national programs, sector modernization, localization, or infrastructure growth
- industrial, energy, public sector, finance, telecom, logistics, or giga project relevance
- operational security, resilience, productivity, and execution issues tied to real change

Weak when the draft treats saudi coverage as a translation of a global message with no local consequence.

## GCC fit signals

Use a GCC wide route only when the issue clearly spans multiple Gulf markets or the source does not support one country strongly enough.

Good GCC routes include:
- cross market investment or adoption trends
- shared risk patterns across sectors
- region wide operational issues
- multi market commentary from a credible spokesperson

## Common failure modes

Do not force a regional angle by doing any of the following:
- swapping one place name into a global pitch with no new support
- implying policy relevance without evidence
- using generic lines about digital transformation or innovation
- overstating media value from event attendance alone
- claiming urgency with no market reason

## How to report weak fit

When the regional fit is weak, say it directly and then offer one of these paths:
- keep the pitch broader
- reposition it around a sector issue rather than a country claim
- use it as a byline or comment opportunity instead of a news pitch
- ask for a local proof point before drafting a stronger regional version
